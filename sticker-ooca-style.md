# Sticker Ooca Style

Use the supplied Ooca artwork as the visual source of truth. Match the reference closely and preserve its recognizable character identity and illustration language. Do not invent a new mascot or visual direction.

## Style guidelines

- Preserve the reference character's silhouette, proportions, facial features, and distinctive details.
- Match the reference's colors, linework, shading, texture, and finish. Inspect the artwork before choosing or describing these traits; do not infer them from the character or brand name alone.
- Keep the character's expression and pose clear at small sticker size.
- Use a simple, readable composition with the character as the focus and enough clear space around its silhouette.
- Keep the overall feeling approachable and friendly, consistent with the supplied artwork.
- Avoid adding text, logos, watermarks, extra characters, or decorative elements unless requested.
- Preserve the reference's background and transparency treatment; check the source asset rather than guessing.

## Reusable image prompt

> Create a sticker illustration in the style of the supplied Ooca reference. Preserve the same character identity, silhouette, proportions, facial design, palette, linework, shading, texture, and overall finish. Depict **[subject or idea]** with **[expression or pose]**. Keep the composition simple, centered, and readable at small size, with clear space around the character. Match the reference background and transparency treatment. Do not redesign the character or introduce a different art style. No text, logo, watermark, or unrequested elements.

## Before delivery

Compare the result directly with the supplied reference at full size and at its intended display size. Check character consistency, palette, rendering, silhouette readability, and background treatment. If the reference is unavailable, describe the result as exploratory rather than claiming an exact style match.
